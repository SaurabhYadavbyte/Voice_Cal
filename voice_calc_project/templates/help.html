Help

Tap keys to build an expression, then press =. Use parentheses for groups. Science keys insert functions: for example, sin(30), sqrt(9), or fact(5). Unclosed parentheses are completed when you calculate.

Deg and Rad change trigonometry units. Inv switches sin, cos, and tan to inverse functions. % divides by 100. Voice input needs microphone permission and browser speech recognition support.
